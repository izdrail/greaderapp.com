## Title

gReader - RSS Reader

## Short description (80 chars max)

Fast, open-source RSS reader. Offline reading, podcasts and voice reading.

## Full description

gReader is a fast, focused RSS reader inspired by the classic gReader
workflow. No ads, no subscription, no algorithm deciding what you should
read.

- All, unread and starred views with fast search and filters
- Offline reading: articles and media stay available without a connection
- Feed discovery: add feeds by URL, browse categories, news and podcasts
- OPML import and export: bring your subscriptions with you
- Podcasts: find, subscribe and play podcast feeds
- Voice reading: listen to articles with configurable voice, speed and pitch
- Reading mode with text size, themes and page inversion
- Folders and per-feed control for large reading lists
- Themes: default, green, sepia, dark and black

gReader is free and open source. Local RSS works without an account;
your feeds and articles stay on your device.
